## **Executive Summary**

Meridian Technologies Inc. projects total revenue of AED 98.4 million for FY2025, representing a 14.2% increase over FY2024 actual revenue of AED 86.2 million. This budget reflects the company's continued investment in cloud-native product development, expansion into the Saudi Arabian market, and a planned headcount increase of 42 employees across Engineering, Sales, and Customer Success.
