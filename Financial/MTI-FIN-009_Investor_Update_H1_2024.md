## **Dear Shareholders and Investors,**

We are pleased to report strong first-half 2024 results for Meridian Technologies Inc. H1 2024 revenue of AED 43.8 million represents a 16.2% increase year-over-year, driven by accelerating demand for our enterprise cloud platform and meaningful expansion of our government vertical.

## **Operational Highlights**

Government Vertical: Signed three new five-year contracts with UAE federal agencies totaling AED 18.4M in TCV during Q2 2024. These contracts are a direct result of our ISO 27001 certification achieved in March 2024 and our commitment to sovereign data hosting.

Product: Released Meridian Platform v4.0 in May 2024, introducing AI-assisted analytics, enhanced RBAC controls, and native API marketplace. Customer satisfaction scores (CSAT) improved to 4.6/5.0 post-release.

International Expansion: Incorporated Meridian Technologies KSA LLC in June 2024. We have appointed a Country Manager in Riyadh and are actively engaged in procurement processes with two large Saudi enterprises.

## **Outlook for H2 2024**

We are raising our full-year 2024 revenue guidance to AED 88–90 million (from AED 84–86 million) based on the strong H1 performance and a robust H2 pipeline. ARR exit target remains AED 78 million. We expect to remain profitable at the EBITDA level for the full year.
