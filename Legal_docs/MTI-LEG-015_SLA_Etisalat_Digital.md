SERVICE LEVEL AGREEMENT

**Platform Availability and Support SLA**

Meridian Technologies Inc. and Etisalat Digital (e&)

Effective Date: January 1, 2024

Document Reference: MTI-LEG-015

This SLA is incorporated by reference into the Master Services Agreement (Ref: MTI-MSA-2024-002) and defines measurable service levels, remedies, and reporting obligations.

**1. SERVICE AVAILABILITY**

| **Service Tier** | **Uptime Target** | **Measurement Window** | **Exclusions** |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Production Environment | 99.9% | Rolling 30-day calendar month | Scheduled maintenance, Force Majeure |
| Staging / UAT | 99.0% | Rolling 30-day calendar month | Scheduled maintenance |
| Disaster Recovery Failover | 99.5% | Rolling 30-day calendar month | Force Majeure |

Downtime Definition: Unavailability of core platform functions for more than five (5) consecutive minutes, as measured by Meridian's monitoring infrastructure. Partial outages affecting less than 10% of users are not counted as Downtime.

**2. SUPPORT TIERS**

| **Priority** | **Definition** | **Initial Response** | **Resolution Target** |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| P1 - Critical | Production down or data loss | 15 minutes (24/7) | 4 hours |
| P2 - High | Major feature unavailable, workaround exists | 1 hour (24/7) | 8 hours |
| P3 - Medium | Non-critical feature issue | 4 hours (business hours) | 3 business days |
| P4 - Low | Cosmetic issue or enhancement request | 1 business day | Next release cycle |

Business Hours: 8:00 AM to 8:00 PM GST, Sunday through Thursday. P1 and P2 support is available 24/7/365 via the dedicated emergency hotline: +971 4 123 4599.

**3. SERVICE CREDITS**

| **Monthly Uptime Achieved** | **Service Credit (% of Monthly Fee)** |
| --- | --- |
| 99.0% - 99.9% (SLA met) | 0% |
| 98.0% - 98.99% | 10% |
| 95.0% - 97.99% | 25% |
| Below 95.0% | 50% |

Service Credits are the sole and exclusive remedy for SLA failures. Credits shall be applied to the next invoice. Credits are forfeited if Licensee is in breach of payment obligations. Maximum aggregate credits in any calendar month shall not exceed 50% of that month's fees.

**4. SCHEDULED MAINTENANCE**

Meridian shall provide a minimum of seventy-two (72) hours' prior written notice for scheduled maintenance windows. Maintenance shall be conducted during the standard window: 2:00 AM to 6:00 AM GST on weekends, unless emergency maintenance is required.

**5. REPORTING**

Meridian shall provide a monthly SLA report within five (5) business days of month-end, including: uptime percentage, incident summary, support ticket statistics, and any service credits due.

**6. EXCLUSIONS**

SLA obligations do not apply to: (a) outages caused by acts or omissions of Licensee; (b) third-party service failures outside Meridian's control; (c) Force Majeure events; (d) scheduled maintenance windows; or (e) free or trial services.
