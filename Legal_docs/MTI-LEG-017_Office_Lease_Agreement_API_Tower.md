**1. PREMISES**

Landlord hereby leases to Tenant Office Unit 1402, 14th Floor, API World Tower, Sheikh Zayed Road, Dubai, UAE, comprising approximately 4,200 square feet of net leasable area (the "Premises"). The Premises shall be used solely for lawful office purposes.

**2. TERM**

The lease term commences on January 1, 2024 and expires on December 31, 2025 (the "Term"), a period of twenty-four (24) months. Tenant shall have one (1) option to renew for a further twelve (12) months at a rent increase not exceeding 5%, exercisable by written notice no later than ninety (90) days before expiry.

**3. RENT**

**3.1** Annual Rent: AED 588,000 (AED 140/sq ft/year).

**3.2** Payment: Payable by four (4) post-dated cheques of AED 147,000 each, drawn on a UAE bank, delivered at lease commencement.

**3.3** Late Payment: A late fee of 2% per month applies to overdue rent.

**3.4** Rent escalation for renewal year: Subject to prevailing RERA Rental Index at time of renewal notice.

**4. SECURITY DEPOSIT**

Tenant shall pay a refundable security deposit of AED 147,000 within five (5) business days of execution. The deposit shall be returned within thirty (30) days of lease expiry, subject to deductions for damages beyond fair wear and tear.

**5. TENANT OBLIGATIONS**

Tenant shall: (a) keep the Premises clean and in good repair; (b) not make structural alterations without written consent; (c) comply with building rules; (d) not sublet or assign without prior written consent; and (e) maintain adequate commercial insurance covering the Premises.

**6. LANDLORD OBLIGATIONS**

Landlord shall maintain the building's common areas, structural elements, central HVAC, and building systems in good working order. Landlord shall provide eight (8) reserved car parking spaces and building security.

**7. SERVICE CHARGES**

In addition to rent, Tenant shall pay service charges of AED 25/sq ft/year (AED 105,000/year) covering building management, common area maintenance, shared utilities, and security. Service charges are payable quarterly in advance.

**8. EARLY TERMINATION**

Tenant may terminate this Agreement after the first twelve (12) months with ninety (90) days' written notice and payment of a break penalty equal to three (3) months' rent (AED 147,000).

**9. GOVERNING LAW**

This Agreement is governed by UAE Law and the laws of the Emirate of Dubai. Disputes shall be referred to the Dubai Rental Dispute Settlement Centre (RDSC).
