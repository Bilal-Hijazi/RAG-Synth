We write on behalf of Meridian Technologies Inc. in connection with the Sub-contract Agreement between Meridian and DataSync Systems LLC dated March 15, 2024 (the Agreement). Meridian hereby notifies DataSync of the following material breaches of the Agreement:

Breach 3 - Financial Exposure: Meridian faces contractual penalties from its end client of AED 85,000 for delayed project delivery, and has engaged an emergency replacement contractor at an additional cost of AED 95,000.

Meridian hereby demands that DataSync, within seven (7) calendar days of this Notice:

(a) Immediately deliver all work completed to date under Milestone 3, including all data, code, scripts, and documentation;

(b) Pay to Meridian the sum of AED 180,000, being: (i) AED 85,000 in client penalties; and (ii) AED 95,000 in replacement contractor costs; and
