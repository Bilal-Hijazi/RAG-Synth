**ABOUT MERIDIAN TECHNOLOGIES**

Meridian Technologies Inc. is a fast-growing enterprise SaaS company headquartered in Dubai, UAE. We build intelligent workflow and document management platforms used by banks, government agencies, and large enterprises across the GCC. With 221 employees and AED 88M+ in revenue, we are entering an exciting phase of regional expansion backed by our recently announced Series B round.

**ROLE OVERVIEW**

We are looking for a Senior Backend Engineer to join our core Platform Engineering team. You will own the design, development, and reliability of the backend services that power our Meridian Platform — serving hundreds of thousands of API calls daily across our customer base. This is a high-impact, high-ownership role reporting to the Engineering Lead for Platform Services.

**WHAT YOU WILL DO**

**Core Engineering (60%)**

Design and implement scalable, high-performance backend services in Python (FastAPI) and occasionally Go. Own end-to-end feature delivery including design docs, implementation, code review, testing, and deployment. Lead architectural decisions for new platform capabilities, particularly in our AI/ML-assisted document processing and RAG pipeline services.

**Reliability & Operations (20%)**

Participate in on-call rotations and contribute to incident response and post-mortems. Improve observability through structured logging, metrics, and distributed tracing. Drive down p99 latency and improve system resilience through proactive engineering.

**Collaboration & Mentorship (20%)**

Partner closely with Product and Design to scope features and provide technical feasibility input. Mentor junior and mid-level engineers through code reviews, pair programming, and technical talks. Contribute to Meridian's internal Engineering Handbook and ADR (Architecture Decision Record) culture.
