**3. IMPROVEMENT OBJECTIVES**

**Objective 1: Pipeline Generation**

Generate a minimum of AED 2.0M in new qualified pipeline per month for each of the three PIP months (September, October, November 2024). Pipeline entries must meet the qualification criteria defined in the Sales Playbook v2.1 (minimum: identified budget, decision-maker contact, defined timeline).

**Objective 2: Quota Attainment**

Achieve a minimum of 75% of monthly quota (AED 675,000) in each of the three PIP months, and 100% of the aggregate three-month quota (AED 2,700,000) by December 16, 2024.

**Objective 3: Customer Engagement**

Conduct a minimum of 12 qualified customer meetings per month, with meeting notes logged in Salesforce within 24 hours. At least 4 meetings per month must be with new prospects not previously engaged.

**Objective 4: CRM Compliance**

Achieve and maintain 90% or above CRM update compliance as measured by the weekly Salesforce compliance report. All deals above AED 200,000 must have a Next Step and Close Date updated every five (5) business days.
